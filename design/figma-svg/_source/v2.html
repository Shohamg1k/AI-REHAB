AI Rehab Coach · second pass · mobile + tablet

The screens, with the camera in them

The camera view is the product, so it is now rendered as one — the patient sees themselves, framed, with the pose overlay tracking their body. Everything else was rebuilt around that: a working type system, real icons, and surfaces that stop shouting at each other.

You can see yourself

A real self-view: the room, the chair you are standing up from, your body, with the skeleton drawn over it. The flat placeholder box is gone.

Live and replay look different

Live shows video. Replay shows skeleton on black — because no video was ever stored. The difference is now visible, not just claimed in a footnote.

Measurements got their own face

IBM Plex Mono carries every angle, rep count, timer and score. Instrument readings read as readings, not as body copy.

Fewer boxes, drawn icons

One radius system instead of 16px on everything, hairlines only where a boundary is real, and stroked icons in place of coloured circles standing in for icons.
