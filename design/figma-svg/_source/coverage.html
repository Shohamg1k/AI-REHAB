AI Rehab Coach · coverage audit · 61 features

Every feature, on a screen

An audit of all nine feature groups in docs/FEATURES.md against the design, plus the twenty-seven screens and states built to close the gaps. Where a feature was already covered, the matrix says which screen; where it was not, it is drawn below.

Audited PRD · FEATURES · ROADMAP · ARCHITECTURE · CONTRACTS · UPSTREAM · STATUS · ADR 0001–0008 Result 61 of 61 demonstrated

Contracts drove the detail

Screens show real fields: FormScore.breakdown, SafetyVerdict.threshold, AdaptationDecision.alternatives, PainSignal.basis. Where a type carries a reason, the UI shows the reason.

Some features are states, not screens

Offline sync, low confidence, a milestone, a compensation cue — these live inside other screens. They are drawn as the states they are, at the size they appear.

Internal tools count

I1 and I2 decide whether the catalogue reaches twenty exercises or two hundred, and whether scoring changes ship blind. Both got a real interface.

Blocked items are marked

ADR-0006 gates consent copy and F6. Those screens carry a visible note rather than pretending the decision was made.

C3

Ask a question

390×844
9:415G ▮▮▮
Ask about your program
Why does standing up over and over help my knee?
Standing up loads the muscle on the front of your thigh through the exact range you use for stairs and getting out of a chair. Practising it rebuilds strength in the position you actually need it, rather than in one that only exists in exercises. Ruth chose it for you as the second exercise for that reason — it is in your program notes.
Do you think my knee is healing properly?
That is not something I can judge — I can see how you move, not how you are healing. What I can tell you is what the measurements have done: your left knee has gained 9° in three weeks and your discomfort reports have come down.
Ruth sees all of it. Ask her — I can send this question with your next report.
Send to Ruth What is a form score?
Hold to speak, or type
Answers are general explanations, never a diagnosis or a treatment decision.
